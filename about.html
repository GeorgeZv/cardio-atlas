ABOUT THE PROJECT

Understand the heart.
One beat at a time.

Cardio Atlas brings a three-dimensional heart and a synchronized cardiac-cycle timeline into one interactive study workspace.

Explore anatomy

Select among 27 labeled structures, rotate and zoom the model, and enable the cutaway to explore its interior. The insight drawer connects each structure to teaching notes and reference information.

Follow every phase

View animated valves, directional blood-flow particles and a conduction overlay alongside the ECG, pressure curves and heart-sound markers.

Study at your pace

Pause, scrub the timeline, step between phases, or choose 0.25×, 0.5× or 1× playback. Layer switches let you focus on anatomy, flow or conduction.

Built for the browser

The explorer uses Three.js and WebGL2. Its scripts, fonts and rendering dependencies are included with the site. No backend or API key is required.

Educational scope

This is a procedural teaching model, not clinically validated surgical anatomy. Geometry, motion and flow are simplified. It does not model patient-specific anatomy or solve fluid dynamics. Clinical review and browser/device validation are required before production medical use.

Quick controls

  • Drag to orbit; scroll to zoom; right-drag to pan.
  • Select a structure in the scene or search list to view its details.
  • Press Space to play or pause, C for cutaway, R to reset the view, or / to search.
