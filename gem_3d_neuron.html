Axon Hillock & Initial Segment (AIS)

Conus medullaris / Ankyrin-G Nav1.6 Cluster
Biochemistry: Highest density of voltage-gated Na⁺ channels (Nav1.6, ~1,000–2,000/µm²) anchored to spectrin-actin cytoskeleton via Ankyrin-G.
Active State Phase Correlation Depolarization

Summated EPSPs cross -55 mV threshold; Nav1.6 activation (m) gates snap open within 0.2 ms, driving explosive Na⁺ influx toward ENa (+60 mV).

Channel Gating & Permeability Kinetics
Hodgkin-Huxley INa = gNa·m³·h·(Vm − ENa). Fast activation (m-gate) opens at -55 mV; ball-and-chain inactivation (h-gate) plugs pore at +35 mV.

Clinical Pearls & Neuropharmacology

Pathophysiology & Disease Correlations
Mutations in SCN1A/SCN2A/SCN8A (Nav channels) at the AIS cause Dravet Syndrome & genetic generalized epilepsies due to impaired inhibitory interneuron firing or hyperexcitability.
Pharmacology & Neurotoxins
Carbamazepine, Phenytoin & Lamotrigine stabilize the inactivated (h-gate closed) state of Nav channels; Tetrodotoxin (TTX) plugs the outer selectivity filter pore.
