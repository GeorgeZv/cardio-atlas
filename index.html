Understand the model.

An interactive, idealized healthy-adult teaching model at 75 bpm. Anatomy is procedurally constructed and is not a clinically validated segmented mesh. Use an anatomical atlas for surgical relationships and patient-specific decisions.

Explore with intent

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, and right-drag to pan. Select a structure in the scene or search list to focus it. Internal structures enable the cutaway automatically. Use the timeline to pause at an exact instant, or step through phases.

What is simulated?

One 800 ms clock drives schematic pressure curves, volume, valve states, ECG, conduction and phase-gated flow. Playback speed changes viewing speed, not the physiological heart rate. Particle density and transit speeds are illustrative, not a computational fluid-dynamics solution. Left/right valve timing is simplified; respiratory S2 splitting is not modeled.

The pericardium is not separately segmented. Myocardial layers and conduction tracts are simplified. Reference pressures vary by source and measurement conditions. Volume presets (EDV 120 mL, ESV 50 mL) are illustrative, not universal normal ranges. Tissue oxygen saturation and cavity pressure do not apply to valves or conduction nodes.

S1/S2 mark valve closure. Optional S3/S4 are timing guides, not default normal sounds. S3 can be physiological in younger people; S4 usually reflects reduced ventricular compliance.
